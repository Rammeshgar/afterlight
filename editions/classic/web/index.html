Where are my settings?

On the desktop, double-click the Afterlight icon in the Windows notification area (beside the clock; it may be under the ↑ arrow). You can also reopen Afterlight from its desktop or Start menu shortcut. Choose Quit in the tray menu to stop it and reveal your normal Windows wallpaper.
