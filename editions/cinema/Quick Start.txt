AFTERLIGHT CINEMA 1.8
Nine cinematic pixel-art worlds for your Windows desktop
Created by Sadeq Rezai

1. Quit an older running Afterlight Cinema from its tray menu before updating.
   Run Afterlight-Cinema-Setup-1.8.0.exe. Install for your own Windows account.
2. Open Afterlight from the desktop or Start menu shortcut.
3. Choose a world, then choose Use as wallpaper. Quit the original Afterlight
   from its tray icon first if it is running.

CUSTOMIZE LATER
Reopen the same shortcut, or double-click the Afterlight notification-area
icon beside the Windows clock. It may be inside the ^ hidden-icons menu.
The normal desktop preview returns so you can choose and customize a world.
Choose Use as wallpaper to put it behind your icons again.

DAY AND NIGHT
Press D or use the sun/moon button in preview for a quick switch.
Open Lighting in the collection or customization panel to choose Day, Night,
or Follow Windows. Lighting applies to all worlds. Each world remembers its
own weather and night palette. Stillness skips the moving transition.

COMMAND-LINE START
With Afterlight closed, start AfterlightCinema.exe --wallpaper to reuse your saved
world and lighting directly on the desktop. Add --lighting=day, --lighting=night,
or --lighting=system to choose the lighting as it starts.

STOP
Right-click the Afterlight tray icon and choose Quit. Your saved Windows
wallpaper is never overwritten. To uninstall, use Windows Settings > Apps >
Installed apps > Afterlight, or the Uninstall Afterlight Start menu entry.
Your saved appearance settings are kept when you uninstall.

EXPLORE
The collection now includes Citylight, City Nocturne, Lunar Relay and Orbital
Foundry from Seneschal, alongside the five original Afterlight worlds. Each
world saves its own appearance. Old preferences stay intact after updating.
Press E to see nine interactions and highlight their objects. Click the numbered buttons
or press 1-9. Press S for settings, W for the next world, Space to pause.
Use Save 4K still to export the current artwork without menus.

ROTATE
Under The collection, enable automatic rotation and pick an interval.
It counts active viewing time and waits while paused, covered by a full-
screen window, locked, in Stillness, or while you customize or explore.

MUSIC
Off by default at every app start. Enable React to playing music in Make
it yours. It analyses all sound on the default Windows multimedia output,
including calls and notifications. It never uses a microphone, records
audio or uploads it. Live PC AUDIO bars should settle when playback stops.
The visual-preview button is explicitly simulated; it captures no sound.

REQUIREMENTS
Windows 10/11, 64-bit Intel/AMD (x64); .NET Framework 4.8 and Microsoft Edge
WebView2 Runtime. Setup checks prerequisites. These Microsoft components
are already present on many PCs; if absent, follow the official Microsoft
link shown by Setup. No browser window, Docker, account or AI subscription
is needed. The animation must remain running to remain your wallpaper.

DISPLAY & POWER
This release targets your primary display, not independent wallpapers on
multiple monitors. Balanced uses a 30 FPS target; Eco targets 15 FPS.
Stillness stops animation. Actual usage varies with the device, resolution
and chosen effects; 60 FPS is a target, not a guarantee.

The app and installer are not code-signed. Windows may show an unknown-
publisher or reputation warning. Only install files from a source you trust;
do not disable Windows security. A SHA256 file identifies an exact download
but is not proof of a trusted publisher.

No internet is required once prerequisites are installed. See PRIVACY.md,
RELEASE-NOTES.md and the bundled third-party notices for details.
